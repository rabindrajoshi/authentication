Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to delete your account and associated data from Juwa777, you may request permanent deletion at any time.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send a deletion request email to the administrator.
  2. Include the email address linked to your Facebook or Google account.
  3. Your account and associated data will be permanently removed.

Contact Email:

your@email.com

What Data Gets Deleted

Upon successful deletion request, the following information may be removed:

  1. Profile information
  2. Authentication records
  3. Wallet/account association data
  4. Stored session information

Processing Time

Data deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days.
